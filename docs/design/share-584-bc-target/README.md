# SHARE-1BC-E3 · wallpaper and copy: eligibility, restricted viewers and Atlas-owned states

**Status: synthetic design evidence for an independent B/C preflight.** Not a Samsung observation, Android implementation, runtime screenshot or design PASS. **Revision E3**, unit [#1131](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/1131), answers the [r2 production preflight](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/1127#issuecomment-7234375657): at 200 % the landscape copy-only menu looked like the existing-only menu because its only cue was 14.33 dp of blank clipped padding, so E3 replaces the cut-off-row cue with a persistent scroll track and thumb (below). Revision E2, unit [#1128](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/1128), corrects the three material findings of the [SHARE-1BC preflight](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/1127#issuecomment-7234010392): access × media × action eligibility with restricted viewers, a truthful generic clipboard failure, and enforced popup margins. First revision: unit [#1118](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/1118), parent [#584](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/584), goal [#97](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/issues/97). The [pinned v3 baseline](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/tree/7857a46f8df06491dd00719303c5236f94b5fd94/docs/design/share-584-target) stays read-only. Flows A and D are out of scope. Every image is drawn from deterministic shapes: no private photo, real location or user record.

## What is proposed

**One viewer More menu.** Live `main` has one viewer overflow (`atlas-viewer-more`) with `AI Edit` and, only when eligible, `Swap person` ([GalleryViewer.kt](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/blob/7857a46f8df06491dd00719303c5236f94b5fd94/app/src/main/java/nl/atlas/gallery/GalleryViewer.kt#L949-L972)). The proposal keeps both, in that order, and appends `Set as wallpaper` and `Copy image`, shown for still images only.

| Item | Shown when | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Edit | still image and `PhotoEditorHandoff.eligible` | existing |
| Swap person | directly under AI Edit, only when the swap port says eligible | existing |
| Set as wallpaper | still image | proposed |
| Copy image | still image | proposed |

More is shown whenever at least one item applies (today only when AI Edit applies). A video has no item, so More stays hidden for videos, as today. Ineligible items are hidden, never greyed out.

### Eligibility: access × media × action

Live `main` has three viewer access modes ([`ViewerAccess`](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/blob/b96b2981a1bf6d3cce2dd59c63558f5c2d137bcd/app/src/main/java/nl/atlas/gallery/GalleryViewer.kt#L1178-L1189)). The library host wires AI Edit, Swap, Edit, Share, Favourite and Trash; [`ExternalViewerHost`](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/blob/b96b2981a1bf6d3cce2dd59c63558f5c2d137bcd/app/src/main/java/nl/atlas/gallery/featureintents/ExternalViewerHost.kt#L104-L111) wires none of them, so today an EXTERNAL or SECURE viewer has no More at all. A caller's read grant is not Atlas's to pass on ([L1055–1058](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/blob/b96b2981a1bf6d3cce2dd59c63558f5c2d137bcd/app/src/main/java/nl/atlas/gallery/GalleryViewer.kt#L1055-L1058)), so B/C stay out of those modes too.

| Access | Media | AI Edit | Swap person | Set as wallpaper | Copy image | More | Other chrome (unchanged) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LIBRARY | still | host-wired and `PhotoEditorHandoff.eligible` | directly under a shown AI Edit, only when the swap port says eligible | readable still content URI **and** a MIME the wallpaper handoff accepts | readable still content URI **and** a MIME the clipboard handoff accepts | only if ≥ 1 item | Back to gallery, More then Details (independent, both shown), filmstrip in portrait and landscape, slab |
| LIBRARY | video | no | no | no | no | **hidden** (as today) | Back, Details, filmstrip in portrait and landscape, slab without Edit |
| EXTERNAL | still or video | no (not host-wired) | no | **never**, even for a readable still with a caller-granted URI | **never** | **hidden; no new More** | Back, Details; no slab; filmstrip only with readable MediaStore neighbours |
| SECURE | still or video | no | no | **never** | **never** | **hidden; no new More** | Back to camera only; no Details, no library |

B and C are eligible independently of each other and of AI Edit, so a library still can show any non-empty subset in the fixed order. No restricted-mode action is invented. [`geometry.json`](geometry.json) carries the full `eligibility_matrix` (all 192 combinations of access, media, readability and the four per-action flags) and `access_modes`; the verifier checks every one of the 192 rows against an independent per-action expectation (wallpaper never without `wallpaper`, copy never without `copy`, whatever the other flags), plus 16 hand-written witness cases, and fails on any B/C or More in EXTERNAL/SECURE, on video, for an unreadable URI, on Swap without AI Edit, on More with no item, and when B-only, C-only or existing-only menus are unreachable.

**At activation everything is re-checked and fails closed** (`activation_rules` in `geometry.json`): if access is no longer LIBRARY, or the current item changed since the menu opened, the menu closes with no action and no dialog; a revoked or unreadable URI gives `B-unsupported` or `C-revoked`; a clipboard failure or unknown commit gives `C-unconfirmed`. No fallback photo is ever used: no cached bitmap, thumbnail or neighbouring item stands in.

**At 200 % text the menu reflows; it never shrinks text.** The menu follows the Material 3 `DropdownMenu` limits: width 112–280 dp (labels wrap at 280 dp, so `Set as wallpaper` takes two lines), rows grow with their text and stay ≥ 48 dp. **Margins:** the 48 dp margin is **vertical only** and is measured from the **safe window** (window minus system-bar and cutout insets); insets are not counted a second time. Horizontally the popup stays inside the safe window with **at least 8 dp** to each safe edge (portrait 200 %: 8.53 dp to the right edge). The generator places the popup by these limits and refuses a frame that cannot meet them (reflow, never a relaxed threshold); every menu frame records its `menu_limits` and measured gaps, and the verifier recomputes them from the drawn surface. When the popup is still too tall, it first moves up, then scrolls (landscape); focus or a swipe brings every item fully into view.

**Explicit item identity at large text.** At font scale ≥ 1.5 the larger popup hides most of the photo (20–29 % stays visible), so the menu opens with a non-interactive row: a thumbnail of the current photo and **This photo**, announced as “Actions for *item*”. The row is **pinned**: it sits outside the scrolling rows, so it stays fully visible at every scroll position, including when Set as wallpaper and Copy image are scrolled into view in landscape. A plain `DropdownMenu` scrolls its whole content, so production needs a popup with a fixed header above a scrolling column. At 100 % the photo stays 62–79 % visible and no row is added. The verifier enforces the rule both ways and fails any frame below 40 % photo visibility without a fully visible, pinned identity row.

**Scroll cue: a persistent track and thumb, not a cut-off row (E3).** A cut-off row is no longer the cue: a clipped slice can be blank padding (copy-only landscape: 14.33 dp of padding under Swap person) and a viewport edge can fall exactly on a row boundary, and in both cases nothing shows that more items exist. Every menu whose rows can scroll now draws, on the trailing edge of the scrolling rows, a thin track (2 dp, ink-secondary) with a thumb (6 dp, ink) adapted from the error dialog's position bar. It is persistent (no fade), non-interactive and drawn outside the scrolling content, under the pinned identity row. The thumb's length is viewport ÷ content and its position is offset ÷ scroll range, so space below the thumb means more items below (top), space on both sides means items both ways (middle), and space above means items above (end). The rows give up a 14 dp trailing gutter, so no label and no row hit target overlaps the cue; rows stay ≥ 48 dp tall and the menu width is unchanged (112–280 dp). Track and thumb are ≥ 3:1 against the menu surface (lowest 6.26:1, light track). **A menu that fits draws no cue**, so the initial copy-only menu (track and thumb) and the existing-only menu (none) differ visibly without metadata.

*Accessibility and runtime obligation.* The cue is decorative: no semantics node, not focusable, not in focus order, not a touch target. Scrolling is exposed by the rows container (vertical scroll range and scroll actions); focus moving to a partly shown or offscreen row scrolls it fully into view before it can activate. Compose `Column`/`LazyColumn` have no built-in scrollbar, so production must draw this track and thumb from the rows' `ScrollState` (`value`/`maxValue`), keep it visible while `maxValue > 0`, hide it at `maxValue == 0`, and keep the gutter out of row hit targets (`scroll_cue_rule` in `geometry.json`). These drawings do not demonstrate TalkBack.

**Partly shown rows are never actions.** While the rows scroll, a row cut by the viewport edge never activates its action: a tap on its visible part or keyboard/TalkBack focus scrolls it fully into view, and only then can it be activated. `geometry.json` marks every menu row `activation: activate` (fully shown, labelled, ≥ 48 dp) or `reveal-only` (partly shown); the verifier fails any partly shown row that activates and checks size, safe insets and non-overlap for every activating row. The annotated frames mark reveal-only rows in pink, and every one is fully shown and activating in the matching scrolled frame.

**Atlas-owned errors, same dialog at any text size.** The title and body form one message that takes focus first (drawn with the focus outline) and is read whole. When it does not fit (landscape, 200 %), the message scrolls with a position bar on its trailing edge while **OK stays pinned** and reachable. OK or system Back returns to the same viewer item. No wallpaper or file is changed; the clipboard is untouched in every case except `C-unconfirmed`, whose state is unknown and is stated as such. There is no automatic retry.

| Case | Frame stem | Copy (title · body) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing handler: nothing resolves `ACTION_ATTACH_DATA` | `B-no-handler` | No app can set wallpaper · Nothing was changed. |
| Unsupported still, or grant revoked before handoff | `B-unsupported` (longest B copy) | Can't use this file as wallpaper · The format is not supported or the photo is no longer available. Nothing was changed. |
| Revoked grant or unreadable URI before `ClipData` is set: no clipboard write was attempted, so "unchanged" is known | `C-revoked` | Couldn't copy image · The photo can't be read right now. The clipboard was not changed. |
| **New:** clipboard service failure, or commit state unknown after the write call | `C-unconfirmed` | Couldn't copy image · Atlas couldn't confirm the image was copied. Try again. |

`C-unconfirmed` makes no claim about the clipboard: it does not say it was unchanged, does not claim success, never reads or restores earlier, unrelated clipboard content, and does not retry automatically (OK is the only action; the person can choose Copy image again). The verifier fails any unknown-state dialog that asserts "not changed" or success. B and the earlier C copy are unchanged from v3. Android owns the wallpaper picker (its own crop and home/lock choice; Atlas has no cropper) and the clipboard confirmation. Copy puts a read-granted `content://` URI in `ClipData`; no persistent grant is assumed. Those system screens are not drawn here (v3 has conceptual placeholders).

## Geometry, focus and dismissal

[`geometry.json`](geometry.json) lists per frame: every active control with id, role, label and visible semantic rectangle **in focus order** (offscreen or partly shown rows carry their content rectangle), the inert viewer controls behind the surface, the surface rectangle, scroll container and offset, dismissal path, return target (`same viewer item (synthetic-lake), viewer state unchanged`) and the fraction of the photo left visible.

- **Restricted and video viewers** (`V-*`): no surface, no More, no menu item; the active controls are the host-wired chrome only, each ≥ 48 dp, inside the safe insets and non-overlapping. A LIBRARY viewer keeps its filmstrip and slab in both orientations.
- **Library chrome behind every menu and dialog:** Back, More, Details, filmstrip and slab, in portrait and landscape (in landscape the photo yields height to the filmstrip above the slab). The verifier fails a frame that drops any of them.
- **Focus isolation:** while the menu or a dialog is open, the viewer (Back, More, photo, filmstrip, slab) is inert and absent from focus order.
- **Focus order:** menu: identity row (at ≥ 1.5×), AI Edit, Swap person, Set as wallpaper, Copy image. Dialog: message first, then OK.
- **Dismissal:** menu: outside tap or Back, no action, focus back on More. Dialog: OK or Back.
- **Return:** every path ends on the same viewer item with zoom and position unchanged (v3 [BC-return](https://github.com/LPStruiksma/atlas-gallery/blob/7857a46f8df06491dd00719303c5236f94b5fd94/docs/design/share-584-target/png/BC-return.png)).

## Reproduce and verify

```bash
python3 docs/design/share-584-bc-target/bc_frames.py --build --render --verify
```

Needs Python 3, CairoSVG, Pillow and DejaVu Sans. `--build` writes SVG and `geometry.json`; `--render` writes `png/` and `annotated/`; `--verify` re-renders each SVG and compares it with the committed PNG. It then checks the eligibility matrix and activation rules (above), the menu limits (48 dp vertical from the safe window, ≥ 8 dp horizontal inside it, width 112–280 dp, active rows ≥ 48 dp), truthful clipboard copy (no "unchanged" or success claim when the state is unknown; no clipboard read/restore; no automatic retry), restricted/video chrome per access mode, and Quiet Frame v3 palette tokens, text fit and ≥ 4.5:1 contrast for every readable text run, ≥ 48 dp size, safe insets and non-overlap of every fully shown tappable control, containment in the surface and scroll viewport, focus order equal to the active controls, viewer isolation, declared dismissal, error focus-first and OK-only actions, menu order and still-image items, the identity-row rule (fully visible and pinned when the rows scroll), the scroll cue (**E3**: a track and thumb drawn iff the scroll range is positive and none on a fitting menu; inside the rows viewport, surface and safe window at the trailing edge; thumb inside its track and wider than it; thumb direction and position matching the offset at top, middle and end, thumb length matching viewport ÷ content; no row hit target in the gutter and no label touching it; ≥ 3:1 against the surface from the tokens **and** from the rendered PNG pixels; synthetic fixtures for no scroll range, exact row boundaries at top/middle/end and a 14.33 dp blank overflow; rendered blank-slice and exact-boundary witnesses in light and dark), a visible photo ≥ 40 % or explicit identity, exact 2× type of each 200 % frame against its 100 % counterpart, and that every row hidden at scroll top is fully shown in the scrolled frame.

Result at this commit: **64 frames** (44 at 200 %, 35 landscape, 6 restricted/video viewers), 199 controls, 1109 control checks, 61 scale, scroll and cue checks, 804 eligibility checks, 471 text runs, minimum text contrast **6.26:1**; **12 menu frames with a scroll cue** (minimum cue contrast 6.26:1) and 14 fitting menus without one; **0 errors**. The menu and dialog margins are also compared with the surface rectangle drawn in the SVG. Mutation checks (not committed) that each make the run fail: letting Copy image through outside LIBRARY; adding Set as wallpaper when `wallpaper` is false but Copy and AI Edit are eligible; dropping the safe-window inset from the vertical margin; placing the portrait popup against the screen edge; giving `C-unconfirmed` an "unchanged" body; dropping the landscape filmstrip; hiding Details when More is shown. E3 cue mutations, each failing the run: no cue drawn (16 errors); thumb stuck at the top (18); a cue on fitting menus, including existing-only (fails every fitting menu); rows keeping the gutter as hit target (36); a thumb in the surface colour (12, caught on the rendered pixels).
