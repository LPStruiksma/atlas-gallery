A permissive weight label does not establish rights in the training material. There is no positive evidence here of a prohibited source either, so REJECTED would overstate the evidence. Private unpaid use is not itself a provenance exemption. Do not admit this adapter to the optional private backend, distribute it, or treat this dossier as a new benchmark authorization. ## Licence and training evidence

- **Adapter:** [card](https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/BFS-Best-Face-Swap) says MIT; the [pinned repository tree](https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/BFS-Best-Face-Swap/tree/2209ae537ac5e883d6ec7db5b7bd6dccf1a0eba7) has no standalone root licence file. The [standard MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) permits use, modification and redistribution with copyright and permission notices retained and no warranty. This is the named licence's text, not a separately published BFS copyright notice or a verified grant for underlying data. The card additionally asks users to avoid public figures and anyone who has not consented; retain that condition as an operator safeguard, not an invented MIT clause.
- **Author's training statements:** in the [training-config discussion, owner replies January 26 and February 5](https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/BFS-Best-Face-Swap/discussions/7), the author describes producing examples with an earlier editing workflow, automating it, selecting the better outputs and iterating datasets with earlier adapters. This establishes a preparation method, not a source manifest. The same discussion includes other users' accounts of their own data, which are not evidence about BFS. The exact source photos, subjects' consent to training and derivative use, source-image licences, rights in intermediary outputs, and which dataset/base revision produced the 4B v1 file remain unstated in the sources read. The card's consent instruction concerns use, not consent of training subjects. The [Klein guide](https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/BFS-Best-Face-Swap/blob/main/docs/flux-2-klein.md) and [community index](https://huggingface.co/Alissonerdx/BFS-Best-Face-Swap/discussions) provide no version-specific rights manifest found in this review. This is a bounded search, not proof no disclosure exists elsewhere.
- **Base-model chain:** [BFL 4B card](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B) and [actual LICENSE.md](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B/blob/main/LICENSE.md) identify Apache-2.0, permitting private and commercial use subject to its terms. Preserve copyright, licence, notices and modification markings when redistributing covered material; Apache-2.0 includes a patent grant with termination conditions and no trademark permission. The [Qwen3-4B encoder licence](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B/blob/main/LICENSE) is also Apache-2.0. These grants cover the respective base works, not third-party training images or undocumented adapter rights. The 9B family is not interchangeable: the BFL card identifies its non-commercial licence. No inference conversion or exact base training revision was independently verified here.
- **Operator safeguards:** BFL's card distinguishes 9B mandatory filters/manual review from 4B recommended safeguards. For 4B the card recommends input/output screening for NSFW and protected content (including CSAM and non-consensual intimate imagery), discusses moderation of prompts and uploads, and describes watermarking and C2PA provenance. It explicitly does not modify the 4B licence. Its out-of-scope uses include harassment, deceptive or unlawful uses and non-consensual intimate imagery. ATLAS should require consent, refuse abusive or deceptive use, screen both inputs and outputs, and retain AI-edit lineage; [FULL_HEAD.md](FULL_HEAD.md) already makes these safeguards a backend precondition. This dossier neither implements nor verifies them.

## Alternatives and decision boundary

No provenance-cleared public full-head adapter was established in this bounded review. The [HeadSwap project](https://github.com/LeslieZhoa/HeadSwap) and [UniBioTransfer release](https://huggingface.co/scy639/UniBioTransfer) do not provide a complete weight/data-rights chain in the sources read; see their existing register entries. The prompt-only baseline in [FULL_HEAD.md](FULL_HEAD.md) avoids this adapter but did not reliably preserve target pose; it is not an accepted replacement. Do not use another BFS variant or a mirror as a provenance workaround.

A consent-first, independently documented adapter is a possible replacement, conditional on source clearance, evaluation and owner approval:

- Data source: only project-owned synthetic adult identities with no real-person likeness, or explicitly licensed assets with documented permission for model training and derivative edits; no scraped portraits, public figures, private gallery images, or BFS-generated examples. Record creation source, asset rights, consent where applicable, generation-tool terms and dataset/checkpoint revisions; keep any real-person material out of GitHub. Hold out independent synthetic evaluation identities. Do not claim a dataset is cleared until its actual sources are verified.
- GPU estimate, not a measured training result or a budget request: BFL's [June 4 guide](https://huggingface.co/blog/black-forest-labs/flux-2-klein-lora) reports approximately one hour on a 24 GB RTX 4090 for a small generic 4B LoRA. For planning only, allow 10–30 GPU-hours for a first consent-cleared full-head feasibility study including preparation experiments, repeats and evaluation; quality and duration are unknown. [Runpod pricing](https://www.runpod.io/pricing), updated November 25 and read November 27, lists RTX 4090 at USD 0.89/hour: approximately USD 8.90–26.70 GPU rental, excluding data creation, labour, storage, tax, transfer and subsequent runs. Recheck price and capacity before any proposed spend; no reservation or training now.
- Any proposed replacement must still pass the full-head, target-pose, identity and outside-mask acceptance criteria in [FULL_HEAD.md](FULL_HEAD.md). No guarantee that the small-model recipe will meet them.

Suggested future owner gate (not requested or exercised in this unit): authorize a bounded consent-cleared synthetic-data feasibility study with an explicit GPU-hour and spend ceiling, or leave the self-hosted full-head route parked until a verifiable public adapter appears. No author outreach is needed for either choice.
