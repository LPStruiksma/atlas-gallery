## Private governance and acceptance checklist

This is a gate for a future authorized private curator, not a request to collect or process records now. The [EU GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/) (2016; Arts. 4(14), 5, 6, 7, 9, 17, 25 and 35 as applicable) separates ordinary lawful processing, consent conditions and additional safeguards for biometric processing aimed at unique identification. Do not assume that body appearance is exempt, that private storage by itself supplies a lawful basis, or that copyright permission supplies consent. Have the responsible controller/qualified reviewer assess applicable jurisdiction, purpose, legal basis, special-category status, withdrawal, retention and any impact-assessment requirement. No legal conclusion or commitment is made here. ATLAS's off-device personal media/biometric access remains human-only.

Before approval, the independent curator must attest **without sharing records**:

- Provenance and rights for every eligible input, including photographer/asset rights and documented participant permission for the specific purpose; exclude unconsented bystanders, uncertain records and minors in this proposed route. Any withdrawal or missing permission excludes the affected data and derived material; retain no inaccessible or unapproved data for later use.
- Human-verified labels rather than face predictions, and independently checked event/capture/camera provenance. Use only the already-defined eligible same-event, same-camera and time boundary in WHOLE-1. Keep the validation and test cohorts disjoint in person, event AND physical camera (not merely renamed camera IDs); check these intersections privately before accepting either split. If genuinely disjoint groups cannot be attested, no quality result.
- Keep the existing evaluation protocol and safety exclusions; freeze the protocol, thresholds and question budget before any held-out use. An independent reviewer should confirm the disjointness and denominator rules from attestations, not receive private examples. No new identity pairs, no expansion to other events or cameras and no automatic identity decisions.
- The private ledger stays with the authorized curator. Restrict access, retention and deletion; keep records, raw scores, identifiers, per-pair results, filenames, images, vectors and manifests out of GitHub, CI, logs and chat. Only non-identifying aggregate evidence may leave the private environment after the curator's disclosure review; suppress small or otherwise identifying strata and report suppression rather than reconstructing them. Do not export the consent ledger or private paths.
- No real evaluation until a single exact checkpoint passes the rights gate above and the independent WHOLE-1 safe conversion/graph-identity, numerical parity, ORT 1.23.2 CPU and device-budget gates. No measured values claimed here.
